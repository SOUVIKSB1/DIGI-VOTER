📚 Methodology & Analytical Architecture

1. Data Pipeline & Verification:

Our pipeline ingests official Election Commission of India (ECI) reports for the 2014, 2019, and 2024 General Elections across all 543 parliamentary constituencies. Candidate affidavits, educational backgrounds, asset disclosures, and criminal cases are compiled and normalized.

2. Feature Engineering:

Key feature vectors include: (a) Historical base vote share anchor, (b) Inter-election voter turnout momentum, (c) Incumbency fatigue penalty, (d) Demographic urbanization index, (e) District literacy factor, and (f) State-level swing volatility index.

3. Multi-Model Ensemble & Calibration:

Predictions are not generated by a single model. An ensemble combining Random Forest (capturing non-linear demographic interactions), Gradient Boosting (capturing swing momentum), and Logistic Regression (maintaining linear anchor priors) is passed through Sigmoid Probability Calibration (CalibratedClassifierCV) to guarantee reliable probabilistic confidence scores.

4. Explainable AI (XAI):

Every constituency forecast outputs an explicit SHAP-style attribution waterfall revealing exactly which factors (turnout, incumbency, baseline vote share) drove the predicted margin and win probability.

⚠️ Academic & Educational Disclaimer:

VoteVision AI is an educational, portfolio, and research platform intended to demonstrate full-stack data science, machine learning, and explainable AI in public policy analytics. All forecasts are probabilistic statistical estimates and should NOT be interpreted as official election results, exit polls, or voting recommendations.
